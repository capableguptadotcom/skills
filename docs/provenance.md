# Provenance and ownership

The maintained product is the eight proposed owned folders under `skills/`. They are authored around our daily activities and the user's stated preferences; there is no automatic mapping from an upstream release to an active instruction. The [language and ownership policy](language-and-ownership.md) distinguishes a tracked upstream copy, an owned adaptation and a project-specific verification profile. The current eight are owned wording; none is a tracked upstream copy.

The previous source inventory, per-skill assessments, simulated arguments, scanner/update tooling and validation records are preserved at [tag upstream-audit-2026-10-31](https://github.com/capableguptadotcom/skills/tree/upstream-audit-2026-10-31). That tag points to the reviewed proposal state from 31 October and is historical reference, not an active candidate queue. The larger inventory is intentionally absent from the current checkout.

Useful ideas encountered during that research include reproduction and falsifiable hypotheses, caller-first interfaces, bounded changes, primary-source checks and observable verification. Their current wording and placement are our responsibility. Source popularity, schema validity and a simulated consensus do not establish that those ideas improve a task.

The later [pstack-first review](pstack-first-review.md) examines all 58 canonical entrypoints and selected public change histories in more depth. It is research outside the discoverable skill folders. Its proposed cases and simulated critiques are labeled separately from actual behavioral checks. The [capability map](capability-map.md) makes the familiar earlier activities visible after consolidation.

MIT notices for the licensed collections that informed the work remain in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). The historical audit contains exact source commits and coverage limits. No private organization bodies or unlicensed plugin bodies are included in this pack.

The current change does not alter installed skills, global host configuration or project instructions outside this repository. It removes the source-sync automation and proposes the smaller daily pack. Session-based improvement is deliberately left for a later design discussion.

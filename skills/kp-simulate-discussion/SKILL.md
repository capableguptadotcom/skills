# Simulate a useful discussion

Name the decision and choose a small set of roles with different incentives or evidence standards. For example, a practitioner tests usefulness, a maintainer tests ongoing cost, and an evaluator asks what could falsify the claim. Merge redundant perspectives.

Explicitly label the discussion as simulated. Have each role answer the strongest opposing argument, not a straw man. Ground factual assertions in inspected evidence; do not invent quotations, empirical results, or statements by real people.

Finish with agreements, unresolved disagreements, the proposed decision, and what evidence would change it. Consensus among simulated roles is a structured judgment, not independent validation. Do not force agreement when the tradeoff remains unresolved.

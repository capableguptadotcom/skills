# Document what exists

Identify the reader and decision the document supports. Inspect the implementation, commands, and existing documentation structure. Put the information in its established home and link to the source of truth instead of creating a parallel manual.

Explain observable behavior, prerequisites, examples, and meaningful limitations. Mark proposed behavior as proposed. For a consequential decision, record the context, alternatives, reason, and condition for revisiting it.

Verify runnable commands where feasible and check links and names. Keep secrets, private transcripts, and environment-specific personal paths out of shared examples. Match the reader's language and preserve exact technical values.

# Test the idea

Establish who has the problem, what they do today, what improves for them, and what evidence would show the idea failed. Look for contradictory constraints and the cheapest way to test the riskiest assumption.

Inspect available project context first. Ask the question that would most change the decision; group independent questions only when that reduces effort for the user. Follow their preferred interview pace. Do not force already settled decisions back through discovery.

Challenge the strongest version of the idea. Distinguish evidence, assumptions, and taste. Suggest a smaller first version when it tests the same need.

Keep a compact record of confirmed decisions during a long interview. Finish with the agreed problem, success criterion, scope, and consequential open questions. Agreement establishes direction, not market validation. An interview request does not authorize building the product.
